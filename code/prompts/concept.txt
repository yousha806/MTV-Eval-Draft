System Prompt
 You are an expert multimodal evaluator specializing in cultural reasoning and visual understanding. Evaluate whether an adapted image successfully transforms the source image to a target culture while preserving the category of the concept in the original image (like food, beverage, agriculture etc.)
User Prompt
 You are given:
Source image
Adapted image (system output)
Source culture description
Target culture description
Category of Source Image


Source culture: {source_culture_text}
Target culture: {target_culture_text}
Category: {category}
Evaluate the adapted image using the criteria below. Score each criterion from 1 (very poor) to 5 (excellent) and give brief reasoning (1–3 sentences) per criterion. Give scores such that for each criterion, a score of 4 and 5 would indicate overall success and a score of 1, 2, or 3 would indicate failure. 
A. Source Image Cultural Appropriateness (1–5)
 How well does the source image already reflect the target culture?
B. Adapted Image Cultural Appropriateness (1–5)
 How well does the adapted image reflect the target culture?
C. Semantic Preservation — Concept (1–5)
 Does the adapted image belong to the same category as the source image (e.g., food remains food; clothes are changed into clothes, and so on), while allowing culturally appropriate changes?
D. Visual & Structural Coherence (1–5)
 Is the image visually coherent and free of major artifacts or inconsistencies?

After scoring A/B/C/D, provide an Overall Success Score (1–5) that reflects overall task success. The task will be considered successful if: a) the adapted image preserves the semantic category of the original image (i.e., if the score for "C_semantic_preservation" is 4 or 5); and b) if the score for the adapted image's cultural appropriateness is greater than the score for the source image's cultural appropriateness (i.e. if the score for "B_adapted_cultural_appropriateness" is greater than the score for "A_source_cultural_appropriateness"). If the model fails to fulfil both of these criteria, assign a score of 0 for overall success. A score between 1-5 would be used to assess the degree of success. Briefly justify the overall score (1–3 sentences).
